**The Family (2026) by Arthur Hovenkamp**\
How absurd,
Jesus Christ calling God His Father as well as the LORD.

To not talk
about how “God is non-binary.”.

For God is
less like a father and more like family. Not a family, of people, or the family,
of the in-group opposed to the out-group, but family in general. The connections
of the parts that make up the whole, “God, that is to say, Nature.”.

That is to
say “God is non-binary.”.

For God is
no LORD, no Anglo-Saxon LOAF-WARD, handing out necessities and luxuries to those
within His hall. Fighting those unwelcome, those ceorls and thralls, those earls
of other halls. God is YHWH, “They were. They are. They will be.”.

“God, that
is to say, Nature.”.

Nature as existence
itself.

God is all,
all is God.

God is
family, the connections of the parts that make up the whole.

God is all,
all is God.

While not
even God is God, no personal entity with a personal name, not singular nor
triadic. God is all, all is God, and that is all. An imperfect word to describe
a perfect condition. Imperfect are the parts of the perfect whole.

Our perfect
family of which we are imperfectly part.
